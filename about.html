Why Am I Interested in Graphic and Web Design?

Back before I attended Combs High School, I am an arts person, and so I was primarily interested in trying out art fields, such as photography and drawings, so getting on graphic and especially web designing initially wasn't for my interest. It's not until I picked up classes on Graphic Design in high school because it was somewhat related to arts in term of designing, and here I am creating posters, logos and flyers for fictional companies, which lead to me learning how to expand my creativity by reaching out sources of inspirations in any ways possible.

The reason I got into Web Designing was because it was part of the Graphic Design field at ASU, so I took it and now I'm having at least some fun creating, organizing, and stylizing pages after pages on websites! So now I am at least knowledge on how Graphic and Web Designing work!
